Tribute: Markiplier

Youtuber Filmmaker Pro-gamer

Summary

Mark Edward Fischbach, widely known by his online pseudonym Markiplier, is a prominent American YouTuber, gamer, actor, and director. Renowned for his energetic Let's Play commentary, indie horror game playthroughs, and interactive cinematic story projects, he has amassed tens of millions of subscribers. Beyond entertainment, he is widely celebrated for his massive charity live streams, raising millions of dollars for causes like cancer research and medical relief.

"No matter how hard it gets, you keep moving forward, because you never know what's just around the corner."

Life and Career Timeline

  • June 28, 1989: Born in Honolulu, Hawaii.
  • 2012: Launched the Markiplier YouTube channel while studying biomedical engineering.
  • 2014: Reached 1 million subscribers and transitioned to full-time content creation.
  • 2015: Hit 10 million subscribers and partnered with major digital networks and platforms.
  • 2017: Raised over $500,000 for the American Cancer Society during a historic 24-hour charity stream.
  • 2019: Co-created and launched the interactive YouTube original series A Heist with Markiplier.
  • 2019–2020: Co-founded the highly successful, self-deleting experimental channel Unus Annus with Ethan Nestor.
  • 2021: Launched the hit comedic podcast Distractible with friends and co-hosts.
  • 2023: Announced and began production on the feature-length film adaptation of the indie horror game Iron Lung.
  • Present: Continues to break boundaries in independent filmmaking, digital storytelling, and global fundraising.
